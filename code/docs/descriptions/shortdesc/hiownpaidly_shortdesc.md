`hiownpaidly` indicates how much (all, some, or none) of the premium the employer/union paid for a respondent's own employer-provided health insurance last year. Universe is policyholders of employer/union-provided health insurance in their own name last year (`hiownemply` == 1). See also [`hicovly1`](hicovly1.md), [`hicovly2`](hicovly2.md), [`hicovly3`](hicovly3.md), [`hicovly4`](hicovly4.md), [`hicovly5`](hicovly5.md), and [`hiownemply`](hiownemply.md).
